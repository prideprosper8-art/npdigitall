PRIVACY POLICY.

How we handle information you share with us. Last updated: September 2026.

1. Who we are

NP Digital ("we", "us") is a technology and digital solutions company based in Harare, Zimbabwe. This policy covers npdigital.in and the project enquiry form on this website.

2. What we collect

When you submit the project enquiry form we collect the details you provide: your name, email address, selected service, and your project description. We do not run advertising trackers on this site.

3. How we use it

Enquiry details are used only to respond to your message — to understand your project, reply with questions or a proposal, and communicate about work you have requested. We do not sell your information or add you to marketing lists without your consent.

4. Where it is stored

Enquiries are stored in our secure systems and may be relayed to our project inbox or CRM through encrypted connections. Access is limited to NP Digital team members who need it to respond to you.

5. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry records for as long as needed to handle your request and any resulting project. If you ask us to delete an enquiry that did not lead to a project, we will do so.

6. Your choices

You can ask what we hold about you, request a correction, or ask us to delete your details. Email npdigitalinfo@gmail.com and we will respond.
